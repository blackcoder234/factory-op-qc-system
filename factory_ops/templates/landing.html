Quality Architecture

Zero-Bypass Safety Interlocks

Quality control is enforced directly by system validation rules at each step of shopfloor operations, ensuring unverified or non-conforming items never advance.

Safety Lockout INTERLOCK-01

Preventive Maintenance Lockout

When periodic maintenance or daily shift verification is overdue, the system automatically locks the workstation, preventing operators from assigning or executing production jobs.

Blocks Machine Assignment
Tooling Calibration Gate INTERLOCK-02

Expired Gauge Entry Blockage

Every precision gauge tracks an active calibration certificate due date. If an instrument is expired, the system blocks recording official inspection readings until re-certified.

Prevents Invalid Inspection Logs
Metallurgical Traceability INTERLOCK-03

MTC Chemistry & SNCR Quarantine

Incoming raw steel heats must pass verified chemical tolerances (C, Mn, Si, P, S) against material specifications before release. Deviations trigger immediate SNCR quarantine hold.

Automatic Chemical Boundary Check
